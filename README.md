## 🎮 About me

Game developer with **4+ years of experience** building gameplay systems, VR experiences and interactive architectural visualisations. I work mainly in **Unreal Engine 5 (C++ & Blueprints)** and **Unity (C#)**.

- 🥽 Building a **bridge-maintenance training simulator in VR** for Meta Quest 3
- 🏛️ Developing **real-time ArchViz apps** with Pixel Streaming and Cesium for [Newo Design](https://newodesign.it)
- 🎓 **Game Design** graduate of [Quasar Institute for Advanced Design](https://quasarinstitute.it/) and **UE Gameplay Developer** certificate from [Rainbow Academy](https://www.rainbowacademy.it/)
- 🟢 **Open to freelance and full-time opportunities**
